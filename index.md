We are **Head Up Studio**, an independent product studio. We build thoughtful digital products that bring people together.

## Our first product: Head Up

Combat sports are about more than what happens in a fight. They are about the people who train together, the coaches who guide them, and the communities that keep them going.

**[Head Up](https://headup.me)** is a platform for combat sports that connects the entire ecosystem: athletes, coaches, clubs, and the professionals who support them.

From your first training session to life beyond competition, Head Up is built to accompany your journey in the sport.
